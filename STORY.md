# vim-shapes

As an engineer, I fell in love with vim and nvim. When I stepped off .NET and into other languages, I wanted to understand this infamous editor. After integrating it into my practice, I felt lightening fast, and it was the key bindings that made it possible. Not just the keybindings though, the key _grammar_. I wasn't just typing with my keyboard--I was _controlling_ everything.

When I became an architect, I had to step out of the terminal and point-and-click through diagrams. Things slowed down, and I was generally uninspired. My whole workflow for the last 10 years, unusable. Lucidcharts, Visio, draw.io, and Excalidraw are all repackaging the same experience--poor keyboard shortcuts, mouse-dependent workflow, and diagrams are treated more like fine arts than science, like rearranging pretty shapes with only relative significance.
